    Voice idle
    Stays on until you stop it. Say "go to sleep" to stand by, "Sky Shine, wake up" to listen again, "voice off" to switch the microphone off. In Chrome and Edge the browser's speech service hears the microphone whenever it is on, standby included.
    Arming, starting and disarming are never available by voice — a microphone hears bystanders, radios and the show audio.
